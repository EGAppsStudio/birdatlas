Bird Atlas turns your eBird sightings into a personal, visual map. See at a glance which birds you saw, where and when — relive every day in the field and share your best moments.

🗺️ ALL YOUR SIGHTINGS ON ONE MAP
• Every location is a colored circle that grows with the number of species you've seen there.
• Zoomed out you get country and province summaries; zoom in for every single spot.
• Everything lives in four tabs: My sightings, Lifers, Explore, Share.
• Places and Species lists: see what you saw at a place day by day, or every place and date for a species, in one tap.

📅 CALENDAR & YEAR CHARTS
• Tap a day to instantly see which species you saw at which places.
• See your bird year in monthly or weekly charts — by species, sightings, days or bird counts. Compare years.
• Filter by species, place, province and date; search by common or scientific name.

🏆 LIFER MAP
Follow the places where you saw each species for the first time, shown as photo bubbles. Your birding journey, told on one screen.

📤 SHARE CARDS
As an Instagram story, square post or PDF: year summary, month calendar, day, trip, species, place, chart and lifer cards. Celebrate milestones like your 100th species. Show sensitive locations at province level only, or hide them completely.
🔗 Or send a link: friends see your sightings on a map with the species list, right in their browser, no app needed. The data travels inside the link and is never stored on our server.

📷 YOUR OWN PHOTOS
Tap a species photo and replace the Wikipedia image with your own shot. It then appears in lists, on the map and on your share cards.

🐦 MAVİŞ: YOUR BINOCULAR-TOTING ASSISTANT
Our friendly Blue Tit mascot answers questions about your data:
"What's my best month?", "What did I see at Lake Mogan in 2025?", "What's my largest flock?", "How many flamingos did I count?"
Maviş runs on your device — no internet or API key needed.

🔒 YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
• No account, no server, no ads, no tracking.
• Your sightings and photos are stored only on your phone; we can't see them.
• Just upload the ZIP you download from eBird — no need to unzip it.

✨ AND MORE
• On first launch Maviş gives you a quick guided tour; no eBird account? Start with sample data.
• Also on your computer or iPhone: birdatlas.app
• Interface in 6 languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French; species names in your language too
• Add and edit sightings manually, with counts
• Explore Nearby: eBird sightings from the last 30 days around any point (with your free eBird API key)
• Optional: free-form chat with Maviş using your free Gemini key
• Step-by-step in-app user guide

HOW TO START
1. Request "Download My Data" from your eBird account.
2. Download the ZIP file from the email you receive.
3. Choose that ZIP in Bird Atlas. That's it!
